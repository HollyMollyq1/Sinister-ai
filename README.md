# Sinister AI

**Independent Cybersecurity Research Project**  
**Status:** Early-stage development

Sinister AI is an independent, self-funded research project exploring responsible, repeatable and **authorized cybersecurity assessment**.

## Overview

The project investigates how cybersecurity assessment outcomes can be evaluated more consistently and communicated more clearly in controlled environments.

This repository is a **public project-information space only**. Internal implementation details, system design and underlying technologies are intentionally not disclosed.

## Why Sinister AI?

Security assessment is not only about identifying issues. It also involves understanding whether observations are consistent, interpretable and useful for improving defensive decisions.

Sinister AI explores research questions around assessment reliability, clarity, repeatability and responsible evaluation. These are **research aims**, not claims of proven technical superiority.

## Research priorities

- **Reliability:** Examine consistency and limitations of assessment outcomes.
- **Evaluation quality:** Define clear criteria for interpreting observations.
- **Repeatability:** Explore documented, reproducible research practices.
- **Responsible operation:** Keep experiments within explicitly authorized scope.

## Research roadmap

- [ ] **Develop:** Refine research objectives and evaluation criteria.
- [ ] **Validate:** Conduct controlled tests and document limitations.
- [ ] **Review:** Analyze outcomes and improve the research methodology.
- [ ] **Communicate:** Share appropriate high-level findings when verified.

These are intended stages, not completed milestones.

## Development status

The project remains under development. No completed end-to-end benchmark results or production-readiness claims are made here.
